# Rimworld Cooking Redux
![](About/preview.png?raw=true) 
<p>Rimworld Cooking Redux is a complete overhaul of the food system. New plants, trees and production chains. This mod removes vanilla meals and substitute them with real world recipes that require specific ingredients. Don't expect to eat paella if you don't grow rice first!</p>
<p><h3>Personal Philosophy:</h3>As a veteran Rimworld player, I grew tired of eating nameless meals on trays. Now, a whole menu is available, from basic foods like grilled steaks to the fancy tiramisù. The player will have to choose what to prepare considering what's stored in the pantry.</p><p>Like always, replayability is the primordial goal. The plants and trees sowed will determine the diet of the colony, alongside the farm animals. Got milk? To encourage compatibility, other mods are patched so they provide more recipes if present. For example, if Vanilla Plants Expanded is loaded, the player will be able to make apple pie, gazpacho, kebabs, and much more.</p>
<p>Every food recipe is categorized as simple, fine, lavish or dessert (new). Meals will provide a small boost of twelve hours, each one unique but according to its category: for example, eating paella will grant a +10 mood like any other lavish meal, but a unique buff in three different stats. The overall sum will be exactly +40, the proper for lavish foods. This mod will not neccessary make the game harder. For players of simpler taste, there is always the possibility to cook basic recipes, equivalent to a simple meal, that only demand one ingredient (any meat --> grilled steak; any vegetable --> grilled vegetables). No mood buff though.</p>
<p><b>Recommended mods:</b> SeedsPlease: Lite by Owlchemist and Food Sorting by Kamikadza13.</p>
